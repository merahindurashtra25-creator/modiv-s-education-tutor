10years of the mission

Modiv’s vision

This mission began a decade ago with one belief: a woman’s skills should never be limited by where she lives. Over ten years it has grown into an effort that works with the government to create employment for women.

Today that same vision lets women teach online from home, with free registration.

Teach more learners at once

Group tuitions,
from your own home.

Small batches. Bigger reach.

Alongside 1-on-1 classes, tutors on Modiv’s can also take group tuitions: one online class, a small batch of students learning together. Families pay less per student, and you can reach more learners in the same hour.

  • Small batches

    Choose the largest batch you are comfortable teaching, from 4 students up to 10–12.

  • Learn together

    Students of a similar class and subject study side by side, ask questions and learn from each other.

  • Affordable for families

    Small groups cost less per student than 1-on-1 classes, so more families can say yes.

  • Your own timings

    Set the days and hours that suit your home and family, then teach your batch online.

Add “Group tuition (Small batches)” when you create your tutor profile. Register for free ↗

A little clarity to get started

Your questions,
answered.

New to online teaching? You don’t have to figure it out alone.

Talk to the Modiv team ↗
Is registration really free?

Yes. Rural women can register for this initiative without paying a registration fee.

What do I need to teach online?

A smartphone or laptop, an internet connection and a quiet corner at home. Our team can help you get set up.

Can I teach more than one student at a time?

Yes. You can offer group tuitions in small batches, choosing the batch size you are comfortable with, as well as 1-on-1 classes.

Can I choose my teaching hours?

Yes. You set the days and times that fit around your home and family.

Is an income guaranteed?

No. You earn from the students you teach, so a complete profile and good teaching help you get more requests.
