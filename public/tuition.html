What is tuition and how does it work on Modiv’s Education?

Tuition is extra, personal teaching outside school or college. On Modiv’s Education you tell us what you need or browse tutor profiles, compare them, and book a free demo class before you decide.

What types of tuition can I choose?

You can choose home tuition, online tuition, group tuition in small batches, 1-on-1 tuition, or classes at the tutor’s own place. Every tutor profile shows which formats that tutor offers.

Which subjects and categories do you offer tuition in?

There are 8 categories covering 146 subjects and skills: school and college subjects, competitive exams, preschool learning, languages, coding and IT, art and craft, music and dance, and sports and fitness.

Where can I find home tuition near me?

We have dedicated home tuition pages for Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula, and you can search tutors in other cities such as Ludhiana, Patiala, Jalandhar and Amritsar from the Find a tutor page.

How much does tuition cost?

Each tutor sets their own fee for 1-on-1 and group classes, and it is shown on their profile. Group tuition usually costs less per student than 1-on-1 tuition.

Can I try a class before I commit?

Yes. You can book a free demo class with a tutor and decide after you have met them.

I am a teacher. Can I offer tuition on Modiv’s Education?

Yes. Create a free tutor profile, set your own fee and subjects, and start receiving student requests. Read more on the Become a tutor page.
