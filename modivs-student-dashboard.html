Getting started

New here? Four quick steps to get the most out of Modiv’s Education.

  1. Open Find Tutors and shortlist the ones you like with the heart icon.
  2. Send a request and book a free demo class.
  3. Check My Enquiries and My Classes to track progress.
  4. Use Messages to agree timings, and Update Requirements (in the menu next to your name) if your needs change.
