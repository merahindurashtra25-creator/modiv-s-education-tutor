How to use Modiv’s Education

A quick, step-by-step guide for students, parents and tutors. Pick your role to see exactly what to do.

From sign-up to your first demo class in five steps. Your phone number and email stay private until a verified tutor unlocks your requirement.

  1. Create your account

    Tap Sign up on the home page and choose I’m a student or parent.

    • Enter your name, mobile number, city, email and a password (at least 8 characters).
    • Parents can register on behalf of their child.
    Sign up now

  2. Tell us what you need

    The short requirement form has four parts: student details, learning needs, tutor preferences and a final review.

    • Pick a boy or girl avatar. It shows on your requirement and in your top bar.
    • Choose class, board, subjects and goals.
    • Set online or offline mode, 1-on-1 or group tuition, and your budget.
    Tip: The more detail you add, the better the tutors who contact you will match your needs.

  3. Browse and shortlist tutors

    Open Find Tutors in your dashboard to see verified tutors. Compare subjects, experience, fees and teaching mode.

    • Tap the heart to add a tutor to Favourites so you can compare them later.
    • Only tutors approved by our team appear here.

  4. Send a request and book a free demo

    Send a request to the tutors you like. Check My Requests to see which ones are accepted or pending.

    • Book a demo class to try the tutor before you commit.
    • Your demo and regular classes appear in Class Schedule.

  5. Chat and start learning

    Use Chat to message tutors, agree on timings and share questions. Once you are happy, fix your weekly schedule and begin.

    Change of plans? Use Edit Requirements in your dashboard to update subjects, budget or timings at any time.

Student FAQs

Is it free for students and parents?

Creating an account, posting a requirement and browsing tutors is free. Book a demo class first so you can decide before paying any tutor.

Who can see my phone number?

Nobody at first. Your contact details stay private until a tutor unlocks your requirement from their dashboard, and our team verifies every tutor before they are listed.

I posted a requirement but nobody has replied yet.

Give it a little time and make sure your city, subjects and budget are filled in. You can also open Find Tutors and send requests to tutors yourself.

How do I log out?

Click your picture or avatar at the top right and choose Log out. You will be taken back to the home page.

Ready to find a tutor?

It takes about five minutes to post your requirement.

Sign up as a student or parent

Build a trusted profile, get verified, and connect with students who match your subjects and location.

  1. Create your tutor account

    Tap Sign up on the home page and choose I’m a tutor. Enter your name, email and a password.

    Sign up now

  2. Complete your profile in 7 steps

    You will be guided through About you, Qualifications, Experience, Subjects, Fees & availability, Verification and Review & submit.

    • Upload a clear, front-facing profile photo. It appears in your top bar and on your public profile.
    • Pick your country code by typing your country name in the search box, then enter your phone and WhatsApp numbers.
    • List the subjects and levels you teach, your fee and your teaching modes (online, home tuition or group batches).
    Tip: A complete profile with a clear photo and a short, friendly bio gets noticed far more often.

  3. Upload your verification documents

    Add an ID document and your highest qualification certificate. Each file (JPG, PNG or PDF) can be up to 500 KB.

    • Our team reviews your profile. You will see the status on your dashboard banner: pending, approved or rejected.
    • Changing your name, photo or documents later sends the profile for re-verification.

  4. Get approved and go live

    Once approved, your profile becomes visible to students and parents in tutor search. Check My Profile to see exactly how others see you.

  5. Find and unlock student leads

    Open New Leads in your dashboard, or browse the Active Leads page, to see students looking for your subjects.

    • Contact details are masked until you unlock a lead.
    • Unlocking uses coins (10 coins per inquiry) and needs an active membership plan. Manage both from the bar at the top of your dashboard.
    • After unlocking you can see full details and message the student.

  6. Chat, run a demo and teach

    Use Chat to reply quickly, agree on a demo class and fix a weekly schedule. Quick replies help you win students.

    Need to change something? Use Edit your profile on the dashboard any time to update your fees, subjects or availability.

Tutor FAQs

How long does verification take?

Our team reviews applications in the order they arrive. Complete every section and upload clear, readable documents to avoid delays.

Why can’t I see a student’s phone number?

Student contact details are protected. They are unlocked after verification, when you have an active membership and enough coins for the inquiry.

My application was rejected. What now?

Open your dashboard to read the reason, correct the details or documents with Edit your profile, and submit again.

How do I log out?

Click your profile photo at the top right and choose Log out, or use the Log out link in the dashboard. You will return to the home page.
