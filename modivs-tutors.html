    Instructions for tutors: your own study materials

    Notes, worksheets and tests you share with students reflect on you and on Modiv’s Education. Please follow these guidelines.

    1. Use your own materialShare notes, worksheets and practice tests that you created yourself. You keep ownership of what you make.
    2. Respect other people’s workDo not share scanned textbooks, paid course content, coaching-institute notes or exam papers you have no right to. Name the source when you quote any.
    3. Match the syllabusPrepare material for the student’s class, board and level. Check answers and formulas before you send anything.
    4. Share only with your studentsSend material to students you teach. Do not put files on your public profile or sell them to students outside a booked class.
    5. Keep contact details out of filesDo not add phone numbers, links or ads to your material. Contact details stay private until a student sends a request.
    6. Protect student privacyNever include names, marks or photos of students in material you share with others.

    Modiv’s Education may ask you to remove material that breaks these guidelines. Not sure about something? Contact us.

    Tutor search FAQs: home and online tutors near you

    Common questions about finding verified home tutors, online tutors and group classes.

    How do I find the best tutor near me?

    Use the filters for subject, class or grade, teaching mode, tuition format and location. You can sort tutors by fee or by recently joined to compare home and online tutors in your area.

    Are all tutors on Modiv’s Education verified?

    Yes. Every tutor uploads a photo, a government ID and a qualification certificate. Our team checks them, and only approved tutor profiles are shown here.

    How much does a home tutor or online tutor charge?

    Each tutor sets their own hourly fee for 1-on-1 and group classes. Fees are shown on every profile, and you can sort by fee from low to high.

    Can I find a tutor for my class and board?

    Yes. Filter by class and subject, and mention your board, such as CBSE or ICSE, when you send a request or post a requirement.

    Do tutors offer 1-on-1 and group tuition?

    Many do. Use the tuition format filter to see tutors who teach 1-on-1, small groups or both. Group classes cost less per student.

    Can I take a demo class before choosing a tutor?

    Yes. You can ask a tutor for a demo class before starting. Check the tutor’s profile for their trial terms.

    Why can’t I see a tutor’s phone number?

    Contact details stay private until a student sends a request. This keeps tutors and families safe.

    I can’t find a tutor for my subject or area. What should I do?

    Post a requirement so tutors who teach your subject can respond, or choose online tuition to learn from a tutor in any city.
