Instructions for tutors: your own study materials

Notes, worksheets and tests you share with students reflect on you and on Modiv’s Education. Please follow these guidelines.

1. Use your own materialShare notes, worksheets and practice tests that you created yourself. You keep ownership of what you make.
2. Respect other people’s workDo not share scanned textbooks, paid course content, coaching-institute notes or exam papers you have no right to. Name the source when you quote any.
3. Match the syllabusPrepare material for the student’s class, board and level. Check answers and formulas before you send anything.
4. Share only with your studentsSend material to students you teach. Do not put files on your public profile or sell them to students outside a booked class.
5. Keep contact details out of filesDo not add phone numbers, links or ads to your material. Contact details stay private until a student sends a request.
6. Protect student privacyNever include names, marks or photos of students in material you share with others.

Modiv’s Education may ask you to remove material that breaks these guidelines. Not sure about something? Contact us.
