E-books and video lectures FAQs

Questions about buying study material, notes and video lectures from Modiv’s tutors.

What study material can I buy in the Modiv’s shop?

E-books to revise from, video lectures to learn from and combos, made by Modiv’s tutors for school classes and exam preparation.

How do I buy e-books or video lectures?

Add items to your cart and place your order. We confirm payment with you, then send your files and lecture links by email.

How will I receive my study material?

E-book files and video lecture links are sent to your email after your payment is confirmed.

Can I find material for my class and subject?

Yes. Filter by type, class and subject, and sort by price from low to high or high to low.

Can I study at my own pace?

Yes. Buy once and study at your own pace with the e-books and recorded video lectures.

Can I get a refund on e-books or video lectures?

Digital products are final and non-refundable once purchased. In rare cases, such as a technical issue that stops you opening the content, we review it case by case. Read our refund policy.
