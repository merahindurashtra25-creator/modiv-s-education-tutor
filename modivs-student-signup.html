Book a demo class: FAQs

What to expect when you book a demo class with a Modivian expert tutor.

How do I book a demo class with an expert tutor?

Answer a few quick questions about what you need to learn. We match you with verified tutors, and you start with a demo class before you decide.

Is the demo class free?

You can ask your tutor for a demo class before starting regular classes. Each tutor’s trial terms are shown on their profile.

Which subjects can I book a demo class for?

School and college subjects such as Maths and Science for boards like CBSE and ICSE, plus competitive exams, coding, languages, music and more.

Can I take the demo class online or at home?

Yes. Choose online classes, home tuition or both, and we show tutors who teach that way.

Is signing up as a student or parent free?

Yes. Signing up, posting your requirement and browsing tutors is free.

What happens after the demo class?

Chat with the tutor to agree on timings and fees, fix a weekly schedule and begin. Your classes appear in your Class Schedule.
