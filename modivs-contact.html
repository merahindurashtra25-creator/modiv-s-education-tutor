Quick answers

How do I find a tutor?

Fill in the student or parent form with the class, subject, board and area. We show you verified tutors who match, and you can ask for a demo class before you decide.

How are tutors verified?

Every tutor uploads a profile photo, a government ID and a qualification certificate. Our admin team checks them, and only approved profiles are shown to students.

I am a tutor. How do I get student leads?

Create your profile and wait for approval. Approved tutors see new student requests in their dashboard and unlock the ones they want with coins.

My profile was rejected. What now?

Open your tutor dashboard to see the exact reasons, such as a blurry photo or an invalid ID. Fix those details using Edit profile and save, and your profile goes back for review.

Can I change my details after signing up?

Yes. Tutors can update their photo, contact numbers, subjects and fees any time from Edit profile. Changing a photo, name or document sends the profile for a quick re-check.
