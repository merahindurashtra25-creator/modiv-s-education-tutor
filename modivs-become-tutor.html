About Modiv’s Education

What is Modiv’s Education?

Modiv’s Education connects verified tutors with students and parents who want home, online and group tuition. Learners browse approved profiles, compare subjects and fees, and book a demo class before they commit.

As a Modivian tutor you build one profile, set your own terms and let the right students find you.

  • Home tuition, online classes and small group batches
  • Every tutor is checked by our team before going live
  • Students and parents across Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula and online

Why become a Modivian tutor?

Along with the joy of helping students learn, you get the freedom to teach on your own terms.

Flexibility

Decide your own timings and availability. Teach when it suits you.

Your fees

Set your own hourly fee for 1-on-1 and group classes. Students see it on your profile.

Teach your way

Offer online classes, home tuition or group batches, or a mix of all three.

Students come to you

Parents and students find your profile and send requests that match your subjects.

Getting started is easy

A few things to know before you begin.

Qualifications

Add your qualifications and upload your highest qualification certificate when you build your profile.

Simple verification

Upload a profile photo, a government ID and your certificate. Our team checks them before your profile goes live.

Privacy protected

Students’ phone numbers and emails stay private until you unlock their requirement.

Membership & coins

To unlock student leads you need an active membership plan and coins (10 coins per inquiry).

How to get started

Four steps from sign up to your first class.

  1. Step 1

    Create your tutor account

    Tap Sign up as a tutor and enter your basic details.

    Sign up as a tutor
  2. Step 2

    Complete your profile

    Add About you, Qualifications, Experience, Subjects, and Fees & availability. A clear photo and a short, friendly bio help you get noticed.

  3. Step 3

    Get verified

    Upload your government ID and highest qualification certificate (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 500 KB each) and submit for review.

    • Track pending, approved or rejected on your dashboard
    • Fix and resubmit if anything needs changing
  4. Step 4

    Go live and start teaching

    Once approved, students can find you. Unlock requirements, chat, run a demo class and fix your weekly schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for tutors who want to join.

Who can become a Modivian tutor?

Teachers, graduates and subject experts who can explain concepts clearly and patiently. You add your qualifications and subjects to your profile, then upload a government ID and your highest qualification certificate so our team can verify you.

How do I join as a tutor?

Tap Sign up as a tutor, create your account and complete the 7-step profile: About you, Qualifications, Experience, Subjects, Fees & availability, Verification and Review & submit. Once our team approves it, your profile goes live.

What documents do I need for verification?

A clear profile photo, a government ID and your highest qualification certificate. Each file can be a JPG, PNG or PDF of up to 500 KB.

How long does verification take?

Our team reviews applications in the order they arrive. Fill in every section and upload clear, readable documents to avoid delays. You can see the status (pending, approved or rejected) on your dashboard banner.

My application was rejected. What should I do?

Open your dashboard and read the reason. Fix the details or documents with Edit your profile and submit again.

Is my ID and personal information safe?

Your documents are used to verify your profile. Please read our Privacy Policy to see how your data is handled. Students never see a tutor’s documents.

Can I set my own fees and timings?

Yes. You choose your hourly fee, your availability and the subjects and levels you teach. You can update them any time from Edit your profile.

Can I teach online, at home and in groups?

Yes. You can offer online classes, home tuition, 1-on-1 sessions and small group batches. Pick the modes that suit you when you build your profile.

How do I get students?

Once approved, your profile is visible to students and parents in tutor search, and you see student requirements under New Leads in your dashboard. You can also browse the Active Leads page.

Why can’t I see a student’s phone number straight away?

Student contact details are protected. They are unlocked after verification, when you have an active membership and enough coins for the inquiry. After unlocking you can see the full details and message the student.

What does it cost to get student leads?

Unlocking an inquiry uses coins (10 coins per inquiry) and needs an active membership plan. You can manage both from the bar at the top of your dashboard.

Can I change my profile after it is approved?

Yes, use Edit your profile at any time. Changing your name, photo or documents sends the profile for re-verification.

Who do I contact if I need help?

Write to tutors@modivians.com or use the contact page. Our support hours are Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

Is this a home tuition job or an online tutor job?

Both. Choose online classes, home tuition, group classes or all three in your profile, and see matching tuition jobs in Active Leads.

Which subjects can I teach?

School and college subjects, competitive exam preparation (JEE, NEET, CUET, UPSC and more), coding and IT, languages, music and dance, art and craft, preschool and sports and fitness.
