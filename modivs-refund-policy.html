Welcome to Modivs Education! We appreciate your interest in our digital products and tuition classes. Please read our refund and return policy carefully before making a purchase.

No refund and cancellation policy

At Modivs Education, we strive to provide the best educational services and digital products to our customers. As our products and services are digital in nature and delivered instantly, we have a strict no refund and no cancellation policy. By purchasing our products and services, you agree to the following terms:

Non-refundable products and services

  • All sales of digital products, including e-books, online courses, and digital resources, are final. Once a digital product has been purchased, it cannot be refunded.
  • All tuition classes and educational services offered by Modivs Education are non-refundable. This includes any fees paid for enrollment, classes, tutoring sessions, or educational consultations.

No cancellation of orders

Once an order for a digital product or service has been placed and payment has been processed, it cannot be canceled. Please ensure that you review your order carefully before making a purchase.

Exceptional circumstances

In rare and exceptional cases, such as technical issues that prevent access to digital content or unforeseen circumstances affecting the delivery of services, Modivs Education may consider a refund or rescheduling of services on a case-by-case basis. Any such decisions will be made at the sole discretion of Modivs Education.

Contact us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our refund and return policy, please feel free to contact us at modivhometutions@gmail.com or visit our website at www.modivhometutors.com.

Thank you for choosing Modivs Education. We are committed to providing you with high-quality educational services and digital products. Your satisfaction is important to us, and we are here to support your learning journey.

Refund and cancellation FAQs

Quick answers about refunds for e-books, video lectures and tuition classes.

Can I get a refund on e-books or video lectures?

No. All sales of digital products, including e-books, online courses and digital resources, are final once purchased.

Are tuition fees refundable?

Tuition classes and educational services offered by Modiv’s Education are non-refundable, including enrolment, class and tutoring session fees.

Can I cancel my order?

Once an order is placed and payment is processed it cannot be cancelled, so please review your order before paying.

Are there any exceptions?

In rare cases, such as a technical issue that stops you accessing digital content, we may offer a refund or reschedule on a case-by-case basis.

Who do I contact about a payment or refund?

Email modivhometutions@gmail.com for payment confirmations, invoices and refund questions, or use the contact page.
