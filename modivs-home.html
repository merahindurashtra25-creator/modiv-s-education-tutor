Why choose online or home tuition?

Both work well. Pick the one that fits your child, your schedule and your area.

Online tuition

Learn from anywhere

  • Wider choice of tutors. You are not limited to your neighbourhood, so you can pick the best match for the subject.
  • Saves travel time. No commute means more time for practice and rest.
  • Flexible timings. Easy to reschedule around school, exams and family plans.
  • Recorded notes and shared screen. Tutors can share whiteboards, PDFs and practice sheets live.
  • Often more affordable. Tutors save on travel, which can keep fees lower.
Home tuition

Personal attention at home

  • Face-to-face teaching. Younger learners often stay more focused with a tutor in the room.
  • Comfortable, familiar space. Children ask doubts more freely in their own home.
  • Parents can stay involved. You see how the class runs and how your child responds.
  • Fewer screen distractions. Good for students who already spend long hours online.
  • Hands-on subjects. Easier for practice-based learning like Maths, Science, Art and Music.
On both

What you always get

  • Verified tutors. Photo, ID and qualifications are checked before a profile goes live.
  • Demo class first. Try a class before you commit.
  • 1-on-1 or small groups. Choose the format and budget that suit you.
  • Switch any time. Start online and move to home tuition, or the other way round.
