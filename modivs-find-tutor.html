What is Modiv’s Education?

Modiv’s Education connects students and parents with verified tutors for home, online and group tuition. You tell us what you need, compare approved profiles, and try a demo class before you commit.

Every tutor on Modiv’s is checked by our team, and your contact details stay private until a verified tutor unlocks your requirement.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for parents and students.

How do I find a tutor?

Fill in the student or parent form with the class, subject, board and area. You will see verified tutors who match, and you can ask for a demo class before you decide.

Is it free for students and parents?

Creating an account, posting a requirement and browsing tutors is free. Book a demo class first so you can decide before paying any tutor.

Are the tutors verified?

Yes. Every tutor uploads a photo, a government ID and a qualification certificate. Our admin team checks them, and only approved profiles are shown.

Should I choose online or home tuition?

Online suits older students, busy schedules and subjects where you want the best tutor from any city. Home tuition suits younger children and subjects that need hands-on practice. You can switch later.

Is there a demo class?

You can ask a tutor for a demo class before starting. Check the tutor’s profile for their trial terms.

How much does tuition cost?

Each tutor sets their own hourly fee for 1-on-1 and group classes. Fees are shown on the profile, so you can compare before you send a request.

Can I choose 1-on-1 or group classes?

Yes. Many tutors offer both. 1-on-1 gives full personal attention, while small groups cost less per student.

Who can see my phone number?

Nobody at first. Your contact details stay private until a tutor unlocks your requirement, and our team verifies every tutor before they are listed.

Which cities do you cover for home tuition?

We have verified tutors in Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula, and the list grows as more tutors are approved. If your city is not listed, start with online tuition from anywhere.

I posted a requirement but nobody has replied yet.

Give it a little time and make sure your city, subjects and budget are filled in. You can also open Find Tutors in your dashboard and send requests to tutors yourself.

Can I change my tutor if it is not working?

Yes. Tell us what is not working and we will help you find a better match.

Who do I contact if I need help?

Write to modivhometutions@gmail.com, chat with us on WhatsApp or use the contact page. Support hours are Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

How do I find a home tutor near me?

Use Quick search above or browse tutors and filter by location, subject, class and teaching mode. You will see verified home tutors in your area along with their fees.

Can I find a tutor for JEE, NEET or other competitive exams?

Yes. Besides school subjects, tutors teach competitive exam preparation such as JEE, NEET, CUET and UPSC, plus coding, languages, music and more.
