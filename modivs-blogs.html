Study tips and exam preparation FAQs

About the Modiv’s Education blog for students, parents and tutors.

What topics do the Modiv’s Education blogs cover?

Study tips, exam preparation advice, board exam strategies and guidance for parents, written by Modiv’s tutors.

Who writes these study blogs?

Verified Modiv’s tutors write them from their own teaching experience.

How can I publish a blog as a tutor?

Log in and open your tutor dashboard, then publish your blog from there. Sharing study tips is a good way to get noticed by students and parents.

Can I comment on a blog?

Yes. Each blog has a comments section where readers can ask questions and share their views.

Can a blog replace a tutor for exam preparation?

Blogs help with planning and revision, but a tutor gives personal attention. Find a Modivian Tutor and book a demo class before you commit.
